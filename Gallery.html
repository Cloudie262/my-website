Graffiti is vandalism and not art. Anyone caught painting should punished. To what extent do you agree.

The key issue of graffiti being regarded as vandalism is lack of authorization. It involves marking or altering property without owner’s permission. In addition, most places where graffiti is being drawn are public areas- parks, walls or bridges. Some people reckon that this ruins the beauty of cities. On top of that, owners and governments have to spend a great amount of budget to clean up the mess. Thus, not only does graffiti damage belongings, but it also causes financial costs.

On the other hand, surveys found that graffiti designed with purpose and permission sometimes light up the area where it is painted, especially kindergarten playgrounds. Children are fonder of playing in colorfully-painted playgrounds compared to regular one. In addition, it can be a great help for creativity if we integrate it into learning and inspiration. Therefore, graffiti itself may be vandalism but with frames and boundaries, positive outcomes can be generated.

In conclusion, graffiti without permission is illegal and should be subject to legal penalties – fine or punishment. However, governments should pay tribute to educationally friendly graffiti and permit them to draw on their respective fields. Therefore, I reckon that disciplinary actions should be taken only if they interpret negative messages.
