Favourite Book

Heaven Official's Blessing (or Tian Guan Ci Fu) is a popular Chinese fantasy web novel by Mo Xiang Tong Xiu. It follows Xie Lian, a compassionate but chronically unlucky fallen god, who ascends to the heavens for a third time. Accompanied by Hua Cheng, a powerful and devoted Ghost King, he investigates heavenly mysteries and uncovers buried divine tragedies
