Wave to Earth
wave to earth (Korean: 웨이브투어스; stylized in all lowercase) is a South Korean indie rock band that consists of lead vocalist and guitarist Daniel Kim, drummer Dong Q, and bassist John Cha.[1] Since their formation in 2019, they have released two studio albums, 0.1 flaws and all. in 2023 and play with earth! 0.03 in 2024.
